01 / A little about me

Technology works best when it helps us see what’s possible.

I work at the intersection of technology and human potential—building products that make professional identity clearer, opportunity more discoverable, and systems of trust more useful.

My practice draws from product leadership, applied AI, and research. I believe the most meaningful products are rigorous in how they work and generous in how they serve people.
